使用支持

折一折手机，决定过山车从多高冲下来。

怎么玩？

把手机像笔记本电脑一样半折：上半屏看过山车，下半屏操作。折得越深，起点越高、坡越陡。找到刚好冲过回环、又能飞过水池落在终点平台上的角度，按「发车」。

有哪些玩法？

沉浸滑道：上半屏立起来就是坡道，球从屏幕顶上滚下来，整屏跟着它往下走。反向滑道：把下半屏抬起来朝向自己，球从屏幕下方往上冲。折叠滑道：上半屏是坡道，下半屏是第一视角。断桥：桥会断开，球快到断口时赶紧折出一段坡接上对面，太缓会撞上断口，太陡会冲上去又掉下来。经典：折叠角度决定起点高度。每种玩法都可以点「?」看折法动画。

普通 iPhone 能玩吗？

可以。没有铰链的 iPhone 会在屏幕上显示滑块，用来代替折叠角度。

折叠时屏幕变黑了？

把手机折得太深时，系统可能会关闭内屏。游戏只用到大约 100° 到 165° 之间的角度，100° 就是最高起点，不需要再往下折。角度低于约 98° 时游戏会提示。

视角、回放和轨迹

下半屏可以切换第一视角、追逐、侧面和导演镜头，跑的过程中也能切换。跑完后点「回放」看慢动作重播，打开「轨迹」可以看到按速度着色的路线：蓝色慢，红色快。

需要联网、注册或付费吗？

不需要。游戏离线可玩，没有账号、广告或应用内购买。

Support (English)

How to play. Hold the phone upright and half-fold it like a laptop. The raised half of the screen is the ramp: fold deeper for a steeper slope and a higher start. Find the angle that clears the loop and lands on the finish platform, then tap Launch.

Modes. Immersive: the ball rolls down from the top and the whole screen follows it. Reverse Slide: raise the bottom half toward you and the ball races up the screen. Fold Slide: the top half is the ramp, the bottom half rides in first person. Broken Bridge: as the ball nears each gap, fold quickly to raise a ramp across it; too flat hits the broken edge, too steep shoots up and falls back. Classic: the fold sets the start height, with four cameras. Tap "?" in any mode for a how-to-fold animation.

Regular iPhones. iPhones without a hinge show a slider in place of the fold angle.

Screen turns off when folding? Folding very deep can make the system turn the inner screen off. The game never needs more than about 100°, and it warns you below about 98°.

Replay and path. After a ride, tap Replay for a slow-motion replay. Turn on Path to see the route colored by speed, blue for slow and red for fast.

Account, network or payment? None. The game works offline with no account, ads or in-app purchases.
